Try Answerbase with your own judgment.

Use synthetic or openly licensed documents only. This worksheet saves a local JSON file; it sends nothing to a server. A real participant must complete it. Automated checks and AI-generated opinions do not count as tester feedback.

Tasks

  1. Create an account, confirm its email, create your organization and two workspaces.
  2. Upload a current policy and an older contradictory policy. Ask a factual question and inspect its citation and original source.
  3. Ask a question the documents cannot answer. Check whether the product abstains.
  4. Upload a newer version, wait for indexing, and ask again. Check that the source date and answer reflect the new version.
  5. Use a second confirmed synthetic account with viewer access to only one workspace. Confirm the other workspace is unavailable. Ask about a group-restricted document and confirm no answer leaks its contents.
  6. Mark one answer Not helpful with a reason. As an admin, find it in Questions and export unanswered feedback.
