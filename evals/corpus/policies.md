# Acorn policies — current, effective 2026-01-01
Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. The current annual policy supersedes the archived 14-day annual refund rule. Refund requests go to the billing team.
Support operates Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. Support is closed on weekends.
Pilot trials last 14 days. Organization invitation links expire after 7 days. Workspace access requires explicit membership.
Exports are prepared within 24 hours. Export download links expire after 48 hours.
