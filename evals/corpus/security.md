# Acorn security and operations
Passwords require at least 14 characters. Administrators must enable multi-factor authentication. Security logs are retained for 30 days. Backups are retained for 14 days. Customer data is stored in the EU region.
Priority P1 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. P1 status updates are sent every 30 minutes. Priority P2 incidents must be acknowledged within 4 hours. The incident commander approves incident closure.
